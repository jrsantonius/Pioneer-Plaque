FAQ

Questions people ask.

How many photos do I need?

It depends on the layout you choose: between 65 and 84 photos. You’ll see the exact number before you pick. If you’re short, empty frames can be filled with photos you’ve already added.

Are the dates next to photos filled in automatically?

No. The date saved in a photo file isn’t always the day it was taken. It might be when it was downloaded, edited, or moved. So you write it yourself. If the file date is right, one tap uses it. Captions left empty aren’t printed.

Can I change the text in the album?

Yes, all of it. Tap any text on the page and write your own. Text left empty isn’t printed.

Can I choose which part of a photo shows?

Yes. Drag the photo inside its frame until the part you want is showing, or zoom in if you need to.

What happens after I send my album?

Your album and all your original photos go straight to our team. We’ll message you on WhatsApp to confirm the order, your address, and delivery.

How do I pay?

After the preview, you pay in full through Mayar, with no deposit. You fill in your name, phone, address, and JNE shipping there. Use the same email you entered on Imazine so your payment is matched automatically.

What are the paper and cover like?

The pages are printed on Art Paper 260 gsm, thick enough that they feel sturdy and photos don’t show through to the other side. The cover is soft cover and the album is bound with binding. It’s A4, 20 sheets (40 pages, printed on both sides).

Will the printed colors match my screen exactly?

Close, but never exactly. A screen gives off light while paper reflects it, so prints usually come out a little darker and softer. If a photo already looks dark on screen, brighten it before you upload.

Can I use photos from WhatsApp or screenshots?

Yes, but they won’t be as sharp. WhatsApp shrinks photos when they’re sent, and screenshots are low resolution, so they can look blurry at A4 size. If you can, use the original files from your gallery or camera.

Which photo formats work?

JPG, PNG, WEBP, and HEIC (the usual iPhone format), in any browser. HEIC photos are turned into high-quality JPEG on your device before they’re sent to us.
